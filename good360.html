Good360: How Nonprofits Get Free Pallets of Brand-New Products

Updated 2026 · 501c3 Made Simple

Corporations end up with billions of dollars in excess, discontinued, and overstock merchandise every year — clothing, furniture, electronics, home goods — products that are perfectly usable but can't be sold through normal retail channels anymore. Good360 exists to redirect that inventory to nonprofits instead of landfills, and it's one of the largest product philanthropy platforms in the country.

What Good360 Actually Does

Good360 partners with major retailers and manufacturers — companies like Nike, Adidas, Gap, Wayfair, and IKEA, among hundreds of others — to redistribute their surplus inventory to a vetted network of nonprofit organizations. Since its founding, Good360 has facilitated billions of dollars in donated products reaching nonprofits, schools, and disaster relief organizations.

Unlike a one-time donation drive, Good360 operates as an ongoing marketplace: verified nonprofits can browse available inventory and request items that match what their organization or the people they serve actually need.

What Kinds of Products Are Available

  • Clothing and footwear — new, in original packaging, from major apparel brands
  • Home goods and furniture from retail partners
  • School and office supplies
  • Electronics and hygiene products
  • Seasonal and disaster-relief specific items during active response periods

How to Get Started

  1. Visit Good360.org and create an account for your organization.
  2. Complete their nonprofit verification process (typically requires your EIN and 501(c)(3) determination letter).
  3. Once approved, browse available product listings in their online marketplace.
  4. Submit requests for products relevant to your programs or the population you serve.

Practical Tip

Good360's inventory changes constantly based on what retailers currently have surplus of. Check back regularly rather than assuming a one-time visit shows everything available — new listings appear as retailers report new excess inventory.

Why This Is Worth Your Time

For a new or small nonprofit, in-kind donations like this can meaningfully offset a real operating cost — whether that's clothing for a families-in-need program, supplies for a community center, or furniture for a new office space. It requires almost no cost beyond your time to set up an account and start requesting items.
