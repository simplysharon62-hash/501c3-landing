Fiber 101

What dietary fiber does

Fiber works in several ways depending on the type and food source.

🌿

Supports regularity

Insoluble fiber adds bulk and helps material move through the digestive system.

❤️

Supports heart health

Soluble fiber can help lower cholesterol by reducing how much cholesterol is absorbed.

🥣

Slows digestion

Soluble fiber forms a gel-like material that slows digestion and can help with blood-sugar control.

🍎

Helps with fullness

High-fiber foods can help you feel full sooner and for longer.

Photo: Elena Leya / Unsplash

Food sources

Fiber comes from plant foods

Good sources include whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, beans, peas, and lentils. Most high-fiber plant foods contain a mix of soluble and insoluble fiber.

FDA Daily Value

The Daily Value for dietary fiber is 28 g per day on a 2,000-calorie diet. Individual needs can differ.
