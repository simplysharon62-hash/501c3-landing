Meet Ana Ogbueze

Since 2016, Ana Ogbueze has owned and operated Project: FULL OUT, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that provides training, performance opportunities, and development programs for Charlotte dancers from all walks of life — helping diversify the local dance industry and promote the therapeutic benefits of dance.

As a dance studio owner, producer, director, choreographer, and retired NBA Dance Team member, Ana has cast over 100 dancers each season and built a platform on one simple idea: push for opportunities.

She is not teaching theory here — she is the person who filed the paperwork, ran the nonprofit, and kept it thriving for nearly a decade.
