About 501c3 Made Simple

501c3 Made Simple was created to help everyday people understand what it actually takes to start, structure, and run a nonprofit — and to surface the free and discounted resources available to organizations once they have 501(c)(3) status.

We publish videos and articles covering real, currently active programs — from free software and product donations to advertising grants — so nonprofit founders can stretch limited budgets further. Every resource we cover is one we've researched directly; we link to official program pages rather than reselling or gatekeeping access.

We are not a law firm, accounting firm, or financial advisory service. Content on this site is educational and reflects information available at the time of publication — always confirm current details directly with each program before relying on them for your organization's planning.
